# v1.18.2 — Show model quota once

## English

In **Models & Quotas**, the App and IDE now show each shared pool’s 5-hour and weekly limits once. The account section keeps your plan, AI credits and expiry reminder. **View model quota above** takes you back to the quota heading by click or keyboard, including when the pool is collapsed for a custom model. If no quota data is available, the same link leads to a waiting message. This change does not affect quota or billing calculations.

Download the [IDE VSIX](https://github.com/AGI-is-going-to-arrive/Antigravity-Context-Window-Monitor/releases/download/v1.18.2/antigravity-context-monitor-1.18.2.vsix), or upgrade the App plugin with `node scripts/install-app-plugin.mjs --upgrade`. See [SHA256SUMS.txt](https://github.com/AGI-is-going-to-arrive/Antigravity-Context-Window-Monitor/releases/download/v1.18.2/SHA256SUMS.txt) for archive checksums. To load the installed update, toggle the plugin off and on in the App or run **Developer: Reload Window** in the IDE after saving your work.

## 中文

App 和 IDE 的**模型与配额**页现在对每个共享配额池只显示一次 5 小时和每周额度。账号区保留套餐、AI 积分和到期提醒。点击「查看上方模型配额」或在链接上按回车，即可跳回配额标题；自定义模型下会展开折叠的配额区，没有数据时则显示等待提示。本次改动不影响额度或计费计算。

IDE 可下载 [VSIX 安装包](https://github.com/AGI-is-going-to-arrive/Antigravity-Context-Window-Monitor/releases/download/v1.18.2/antigravity-context-monitor-1.18.2.vsix)，App 可在仓库目录运行 `node scripts/install-app-plugin.mjs --upgrade`。归档校验值见 [SHA256SUMS.txt](https://github.com/AGI-is-going-to-arrive/Antigravity-Context-Window-Monitor/releases/download/v1.18.2/SHA256SUMS.txt)。安装后在 App 中关闭再开启插件，或在 IDE 中保存工作后执行 **Developer: Reload Window**。

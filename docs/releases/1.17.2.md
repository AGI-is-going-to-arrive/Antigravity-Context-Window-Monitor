# Release 1.17.2 — Status bar redesign and Neon models

## English

Hover over the bottom-right monitor to see the current session, context usage, account plan, and quota at a glance. Open Overview, Models, or Settings directly from the hover. Clicking the status bar opens Overview, where context, quota, reset times, and AI credits are arranged for quick comparison. The full monitoring panel remains available from the same entry point.

The hover follows the IDE theme. Choose Automatic, Compact, or Full density in Settings. Overview adapts to a narrow editor group and includes expandable diagnostics for token counts, compression, and image activity. The monitor supports English, Chinese, and bilingual display modes.

### Neon model catalog

The running Antigravity IDE reported these identities on 2026-11-19. Orchestrator (Neon) occupies a single picker entry, while Gemini 4 Neon exposes four independent effort levels.

| Picker entry | Internal ID | Catalog ID | Thinking level |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Gemini 4 Neon Low | `M534` | `gemini-4-neon-low` | 1 |
| Gemini 4 Neon Medium | `M533` | `gemini-4-neon-medium` | 2 |
| Gemini 4 Neon High | `M532` | `gemini-4-neon-high` | 3 |
| Gemini 4 Neon Extra | `M535` | `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` | 100 |
| Orchestrator (Neon) | `M537` | `gemini-pro-orchestrator-neon` | 100 |

`Mxxx` abbreviates `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_Mxxx`. The five entries reported 1,048,576 tokens of model context, a maximum of 262,144 output tokens, a 512,000-token compression limit, and a 50,000-token checkpoint threshold. The startup fallback for the new entries is 511,000 tokens until live compression metadata is available. The 32-token minimum thinking budget describes a model capability. The IDE label **Extra** corresponds to the backend label **XHigh**.

Orchestrator (Neon) is configured to use the catalog-only `M669` / `gemini-flash-worker-agy` worker, also used by the Carbon and Argon coordinators. The three coordinators, the worker, and Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Carbon, and Argon report the same 512,000-token compression limit. Usage and custom pricing follow the actual model call identity. The standalone Neon rates remain unverified and are shown as **Price unavailable** unless you supply a custom price.

The checked account exposed 27 picker entries, with 20 in the Gemini pool and seven in Claude / GPT. The Neon identities share Gemini quota. The live account catalog determines which entries appear.

### Installation

The universal VSIX is 8,088,859 bytes. SHA-256 `62def476b47fd57bb99d6c5f4c729b4f1e664a3cabf1abe3957bc1243ce16939`.

Use **Extensions: Install from VSIX...** to select `antigravity-context-monitor-1.17.2.vsix`, then reload the IDE window. Confirm **1.17.2** in extension details and About. Open Models to check the entries offered by your account. The Command Palette shortcut is `Cmd+Shift+P` on macOS and `Ctrl+Shift+P` on Windows/Linux.

### Neon 模型目录

2026-11-19 在运行中的 Antigravity IDE 目录核实以下身份。Orchestrator (Neon) 有一个选择器条目，Gemini 4 Neon 提供四档独立思考力度。

| 选择器条目 | 内部 ID | Catalog ID | 思考力度 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Gemini 4 Neon Low | `M534` | `gemini-4-neon-low` | 1 |
| Gemini 4 Neon Medium | `M533` | `gemini-4-neon-medium` | 2 |
| Gemini 4 Neon High | `M532` | `gemini-4-neon-high` | 3 |
| Gemini 4 Neon Extra | `M535` | `gemini-4-neon-xhigh` | 100 |
| Orchestrator (Neon) | `M537` | `gemini-pro-orchestrator-neon` | 100 |

`Mxxx` 是 `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_Mxxx` 的简写。五个条目均报告 1,048,576 token 的模型上下文、262,144 token 的最大输出、512,000 token 的压缩上限，以及 50,000 token 的检查点阈值。实时压缩参数到达前，新条目启动时使用 511,000 token 的兜底值。最小思考预算 32 token 表示模型能力边界。IDE 里的 **Extra** 对应后端标签 **XHigh**。

Orchestrator (Neon) 配置的执行模型为仅见于目录的 `M669` / `gemini-flash-worker-agy`，Carbon 与 Argon 协调模型也使用它。三个协调模型、执行模型及 Gemini 4 Hydrogen、Carbon、Argon 均报告 512,000 token 的压缩上限。用量与自定义费率按实际调用的模型身份分别记录。Neon 独立费率尚未核实，未设置自定义价格时将显示**价格待确认**。

本次核对的账号有 27 个可选条目，Gemini 池 20 个，Claude / GPT 池七个。Neon 各身份共用 Gemini 配额。显示条目以当前账号的实时目录为准。

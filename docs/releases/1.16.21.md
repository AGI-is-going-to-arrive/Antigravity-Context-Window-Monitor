# 1.16.21 — Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Release date: 2026-11-13

## English

Antigravity Context Window Monitor 1.16.21 adds **Gemini 4 Hydrogen Low / Medium / High / Extra** while preserving Carbon, Argon, older model identities, account-specific availability, and saved usage.

### Verified Hydrogen identities and limits

The running Antigravity IDE's `GetUserStatus` and `GetAvailableModels` responses were checked on 2026-11-13:

| IDE menu effort | Backend label | Internal ID | Catalog ID | Thinking level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Low | Low | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M527` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-low` | 1 |
| Medium | Medium | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M526` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-medium` | 2 |
| High | High | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M525` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` | 3 |
| Extra | XHigh | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M528` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-xhigh` | 100 |

**Extra is the IDE menu name for XHigh, not a fifth effort or thinking level 4.** All four report 1,048,576 model-context tokens, 262,144 maximum output tokens, a 256,000 platform compression limit, and a separate 50,000 checkpoint threshold. Before live limits arrive, the static compression fallback is 255,000. Thinking is enabled; the reported minimum budget of 32 is a capability boundary, not a fixed per-request budget.

These values come from the IDE and are not public API guarantees. English, Chinese, catalog, and placeholder aliases preserve each effort. Extra / XHigh / Extra High / 超高 resolve to the same fourth tier; unsupported efforts, concatenated names, and unmatched parentheses do not acquire known identities or prices. Family-only `gemini-4-hydrogen` responses retain a known requested tier rather than binding the family permanently to the first observed effort. Hydrogen, Carbon, and Argon remain separate in accounting, history, and custom pricing. An account-specific display-name change no longer drops a known model's built-in price: pricing recovers its verified catalog identity while preserving explicit custom display-name overrides.

### Available models and quota

The checked local account exposes **21 chat entries**: 20 known effort variants and an independent **Orchestrator (`M667`)** entry. Hydrogen joins the Gemini shared pool:

| Pool / entry | Available families and efforts | Count |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Gemini | Hydrogen: Low / Medium / High / Extra; Carbon, Argon, Flash: Low / Medium / High each | 13 |
| Claude / GPT | Opus, Sonnet: Low / Medium / High each; GPT-OSS: Medium | 7 |
| Orchestrator | Independent entry; no inferred shared-pool identity | 1 |

Quota percentages are not summed or averaged. Differing values retain ranges and per-model details; missing data remains unknown. A matching quota reading alone does not establish a new model's pool membership. Dynamic grouping requires both an effort-specific branded catalog ID and its matching provider; `gemini-pro-orchestrator` therefore stays independent, while compatible future or renumbered effort tiers remain supported. The separate image-generation catalog and its existing metering limitations are preserved. Model Info, per-call statistics, and prices keep each effort's identity.

Cards follow the signed-in account's actual catalog, including partial and empty catalogs. Tooltip selection preserves the current model and live recommendation before generation order; Hydrogen precedes Carbon and Argon when those priorities do not apply.

### Official reference pricing

[Google AI Studio Standard API pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing#gemini-4-hydrogen), checked 2026-11-13, lists these Hydrogen rates for all four efforts, in USD per million tokens:

| Input | Output, including thinking | Implicit cache reads |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $1.50 | $7.50 | $0.075 |

Explicit caching is unsupported. Hydrogen uses its own fixed reference rate; Carbon/Argon introductory rates and their December cutover do not apply. Custom family and effort prices retain precedence, and existing archived costs remain preserved. Unsupported identities stay unpriced rather than being treated as free. These are Standard API estimates, not Antigravity subscription charges, credits, or Batch/Flex/Priority rates.

### Hydrogen 实测身份与限额

2026-11-13 核对了运行中 Antigravity IDE 的 `GetUserStatus` 与 `GetAvailableModels` 响应：

| IDE 菜单档位 | 后端标签 | 内部 ID | Catalog ID | 思考力度 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Low | Low | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M527` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-low` | 1 |

| High | High | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M525` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-high` | 3 |
| Extra | XHigh | `MODEL_PLACEHOLDER_M528` | `gemini-4-hydrogen-xhigh` | 100 |

**Extra 是 XHigh 在 IDE 菜单中的名称，不是第五档，思考力度也不是 4。** 四档均报告模型上下文 1,048,576 token、最大输出 262,144、平台压缩上限 256,000，以及独立的 50,000 检查点阈值。实时限额到达前，静态压缩兜底为 255,000。模型支持思考；报告的最小预算 32 表示能力边界，不是每次请求的固定预算。

这些数值来自 IDE，不是公开 API 保证。中英文标签、catalog 与 placeholder 别名均保留档位身份；Extra / XHigh / Extra High / 超高 对应同一第四档。不支持的档位、连写名称及未配对括号不能误套已知身份或价格。仅返回 `gemini-4-hydrogen` 家族名时保留已知请求档位，不将家族永久绑定到最先出现的一档。Hydrogen、Carbon、Argon 在记账、历史及自定义计价中保持独立。修复账号自定义显示名使已知模型丢失内置价格的问题：通过已验证 catalog 身份恢复费率，同时保留显式自定义显示名价格的优先级。

### 可用模型与配额

本地账号实测提供 **21 个聊天条目**：20 个已知思考档位及独立的 **Orchestrator（`M667`）**。Hydrogen 归入 Gemini 共享池：

| 池 / 条目 | 实际可用家族与档位 | 数量 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Gemini | Hydrogen：Low / Medium / High / Extra；Carbon、Argon、Flash：各 Low / Medium / High | 13 |
| Claude / GPT | Opus、Sonnet：各 Low / Medium / High；GPT-OSS：Medium | 7 |
| Orchestrator | 独立条目，不推断共享池归属 | 1 |

配额百分比不求和、不取平均；读数不同保留范围与逐模型明细，缺失数据明确显示未知。配额读数相同不能单独证明新模型的共享池归属。动态归类须同时满足明确档位的家族 catalog ID 和匹配提供商；因此 `gemini-pro-orchestrator` 保持独立，同时兼容未来或重新编号的正规档位。独立图片生成目录及已有计量限制保持不变。模型信息、调用统计与价格继续保留每档身份。

卡片遵循登录账号的实际目录，包括部分档位和空目录。Tooltip 先选择当前模型及实时推荐模型，再按世代排序；前两项不适用时，Hydrogen 排在 Carbon、Argon 之前。
